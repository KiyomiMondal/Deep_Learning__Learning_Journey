# Recursive Networks

## Overview

Recursive Neural Networks process hierarchical structures.

Unlike RNNs:

RNN → Sequence

Recursive Net → Tree

---

## Motivation

Many real-world structures are hierarchical.

Examples:

- Sentences
- Parse Trees
- XML Documents

---

## Architecture

Child Nodes
↓
Combine
↓
Parent Node

---

## Applications

### Natural Language Processing

Sentence parsing.

### Scene Understanding

Object hierarchy analysis.

---

## Advantages

- Captures hierarchy
- Learns structural information

---

## Disadvantages

- Computationally expensive
- Difficult training

---

## Summary

Recursive networks are useful when data naturally forms trees or hierarchies.

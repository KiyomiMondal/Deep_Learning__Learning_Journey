# Representational Power

## Overview

Representational Power refers to the ability of a neural network to represent complex functions.

---

## Why Important?

Some tasks require simple functions.

Others require highly complex functions.

The network must have enough capacity.

---

## Universal Approximation Theorem

A neural network with one hidden layer can approximate any continuous function.

---

## Feature Hierarchies

Deep networks learn:

Layer 1 → Edges

Layer 2 → Shapes

Layer 3 → Parts

Layer 4 → Objects

---

## Capacity

Higher capacity means:

- More complex functions
- Better learning ability

Too much capacity may cause overfitting.

---

## Factors Affecting Power

### Depth

More layers.

### Width

More neurons.

### Activation Functions

Introduce non-linearity.

---

## Summary

Representational power determines how complex a function a network can learn.

# Factor Analysis

## Overview

Factor Analysis is a statistical method used to explain observed variables using a smaller number of hidden variables called factors.

---

## Motivation

Many variables may be influenced by common hidden causes.

Example:

Exam scores may depend on:

- Intelligence
- Preparation
- Motivation

These hidden causes are factors.

---

## Concepts

### Observed Variables

Variables we measure directly.

### Latent Variables

Hidden variables influencing observations.

---

## Goal

Reduce many variables into a smaller set of meaningful factors.

---

## Mathematical Idea

Observed Data = Common Factors + Noise

---

## Difference from PCA

PCA:
- Maximizes variance

Factor Analysis:
- Models hidden causes

---

## Applications

- Psychology
- Social Science
- Market Research

---

## Advantages

- Finds hidden structures
- Reduces complexity

---

## Summary

Factor Analysis explains observed variables through underlying latent factors.

# Independent Component Analysis (ICA)

## Overview

ICA is a technique used to separate mixed signals into independent source signals.

---

## Motivation

Suppose two people are talking simultaneously.

Microphones record mixtures of both voices.

ICA attempts to recover individual voices.

---

## Cocktail Party Problem

Classic ICA example.

Input:

Mixed Signals

Output:

Independent Signals

---

## Key Assumption

Source signals are statistically independent.

---

## Difference from PCA

PCA:
- Finds uncorrelated components

ICA:
- Finds independent components

---

## Applications

### Audio Processing

Voice separation

### Medical Imaging

EEG signal analysis

### Image Processing

Feature extraction

---

## Advantages

- Effective signal separation
- Useful for blind source separation

---

## Summary

ICA separates mixed observations into independent underlying sources.
